A Highland Inn on the Sound of Islay.

A picturesque, family run Highland Inn on the shores of the Sound of Islay, overlooking the pier at which daily car ferries berth on arrival from the Scottish mainland.

01Stay

The best coastal location in Islay

Situated in arguably the best coastal location in Islay, this old drove inn offers a mix of rooms, all with en-suite facilities. The hotel has been in family ownership for over 50 years.

The Annex

03Explore Islay

An ideal base to explore Islay

Sea or loch fishing, cycling, walking, swimming from fabulous beaches, the Malt Whisky Trail, ancient history or the Clans and Lords of the Isles — or simply enjoying the fabulous scenery. Islay has it all.

iDistillery & sightseeing tours

We can now book, subject to availability, tours with a local driver who can bring the island to life — by car for 1–4 guests or by minibus for 8–15.

iiFly fishing with John Mc Callum

A locally based professional fly fishing guide, suitable for both experienced and novice anglers.

iiiWildlife, sea fishing & scenic boat tours

Very experienced, knowledgeable skippers ensure you have a safe and memorable trip.

ivFree camping by the sea

Limited free camping may be available — contact the owners through Port Askaig Stores, at the Hotel, or call ahead on 01496 840 245.
